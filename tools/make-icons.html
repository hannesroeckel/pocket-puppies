Pocket Puppies — icon generator

The home-screen icons are drawn in code, here, and committed as real PNGs in /icons. iOS does not reliably use SVG or data: manifest icons for an installed web app — a missing raster apple-touch-icon is why an installed PWA can end up with a blank tile or a screenshot of the page. So: real files, right sizes, no network.

The face is deliberately breed-neutral (soft floppy ears, curly-suggestive silhouette). The breed art is being reworked in parallel and the icon must not encode any rig geometry. Regenerate with tools/make-icons.py, or click the links below.
